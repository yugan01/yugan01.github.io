About

Hey, I’m Yugan. I’m a final-year Double Degree student at the National University of Singapore (NUS), pursuing Computer Science and Life Sciences. I’m particularly interested in the intersection of machine learning, biology, and healthcare, and in using computational methods to tackle problems with real-world clinical impact.

Over the past few years, I’ve had the opportunity to work on several healthcare-focused machine learning projects. At the Singapore-ETH Centre, I worked on predicting fall risk in older adults using wearable and clinical data, with a particular focus on building models that remain useful in the presence of missing information. I have also worked on applying machine learning to wound healing, giving me experience with another area where computational approaches can help make sense of complex clinical data.

For my Computer Science Final Year Project, I explored how large language models and retrieval-augmented generation could be used to provide personalised exercise recommendations for children with obesity, with an emphasis on making the recommendations reliable and clinically appropriate.

I’m excited to build a career in computational biology and healthcare AI, developing machine-learning systems that turn complex biological and clinical data into useful real-world tools.
